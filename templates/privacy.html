Privacy policy

How Votogram handles account and voting data.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Short version

We collect account details, poll activity, and technical data needed to run the voting platform, protect access, and show results correctly.

1. Information we collect

Votogram may collect your name, email address, phone number, password credentials, profile photo, poll creation details, vote participation records, timestamps, and basic technical data such as browser and device information.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create accounts, authenticate sessions, connect voters with poll keys, record submitted votes, display closed-poll results, troubleshoot support requests, and improve reliability.

3. Voting records

The platform stores poll and vote data so each poll can be counted and reviewed. Poll creators and participants may see result totals when the poll is closed, depending on the app flow.

4. Security

We use account sessions, database access controls, and deployment environment secrets to protect the application. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should keep passwords private and report suspicious activity.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information when required to operate hosting, database, or support services, or when required by law.

6. Your choices

You can update profile details from your account page. For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Votogram team with the account email connected to your request.
