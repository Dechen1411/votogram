Our Story

Founded in 2025 at Gyalpozhing College of Information Technology, Votogram began as a student project to address the challenges of traditional voting systems in Bhutan's remote communities.

What started as a simple prototype has grown into Bhutan's most trusted online voting platform, now serving organizations, educational institutions, and community groups across the country.

Our team of passionate developers and democracy advocates continue to innovate, making secure digital voting accessible to all.

Our Mission

"To empower communities with transparent, secure, and accessible voting technology that strengthens democratic participation."
