Why it exists

Less confusion around online voting.

The app focuses on the moments that matter most: creating a poll, joining the right poll, voting once, and checking results when the poll is done.

Account-based access

Voters sign in before using poll keys, giving organizers a steadier participation flow.

Poll key entry

Each poll can be shared through a key, so participants land on the exact ballot they need.

Closed-poll results

Results are treated as a final review surface once the active voting period is over.

Product principles

Designed around voter trust.

01

Make choices obvious

Ballots should be readable, direct, and hard to misinterpret.

02

Keep organizers in control

Poll creation, sharing, and result review should stay close together.

03

Use calm security

The interface should feel serious without making voting feel complicated.
