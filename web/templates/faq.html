Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Votogram

To create an account:

  1. Click "Register" in the top navigation
  2. Fill in your full name, email, and phone number
  3. Create a secure password
  4. Verify your email address
  5. Complete your profile setup

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a password reset link valid for 24 hours.

After logging in:

  1. Click "Create Poll" in your dashboard
  2. Enter your poll question
  3. Add at least 2 options
  4. Set voting parameters (duration, visibility, etc.)
  5. Share the poll key with participants

This depends on the poll settings:

  • Single-vote polls: Votes cannot be changed once submitted
  • Adjustable polls: You can change your vote until the poll closes
  • Anonymous polls: Votes cannot be changed to preserve anonymity

The poll creator determines these settings when creating the poll.

We use multiple security measures:

  • Phone number verification for account creation
  • One-account-per-phone enforcement
  • Vote encryption and blockchain-based verification
  • IP address tracking for suspicious activity
  • Manual review for high-stakes elections

Votogram works on all modern browsers including:

  • Google Chrome (latest 3 versions)
  • Mozilla Firefox (latest 3 versions)
  • Safari (latest 3 versions)
  • Microsoft Edge (latest 3 versions)

For best performance, we recommend using the latest browser version available.

Yes! Votogram has native apps for:

  • iOS (App Store)
  • Android (Google Play Store)

Our web version is also fully responsive and works on all mobile browsers.
