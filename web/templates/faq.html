Voting help

Answers for creating polls, entering keys, and reading results.

Yes. Votogram uses signed-in accounts so poll access and voting activity can be tied to a known session.

Yes. After logging in, open Profile to update your name, phone number, photo, or password.

Log in, open the dashboard, enter your poll question, add the voting options, set a closing time, and create the poll. The dashboard gives you a poll key to share with participants.

Participants log in, enter the shared poll key on the dashboard, and then choose from the ballot options for that poll.

The voting flow is designed around one submitted vote per user for a poll.

Results are shown from the results page after a poll is closed. Active polls stay focused on voting.

Use the Results link in the dashboard navigation to review polls that have closed.

A poll key sends voters to the intended ballot without making them browse through unrelated polls.

Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. The interface is responsive, so it works on desktop and mobile browsers.

Still stuck? Contact support with your poll key and account email.
