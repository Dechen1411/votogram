Terms of use

The rules for creating polls, joining ballots, and using Votogram.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Short version

Use Votogram honestly, protect your account, do not manipulate polls, and respect the voting process created by each poll owner.

1. Acceptance

By accessing or using Votogram, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

2. Service

Votogram provides web-based tools for creating polls, sharing poll keys, collecting votes, and viewing results after polls close.

3. Accounts

You are responsible for the accuracy of your account information and the security of your password. Do not share account access with another person for voting purposes.

4. Voting conduct

Do not attempt to submit unauthorized votes, bypass poll restrictions, interfere with another voter, scrape private poll data, or manipulate results.

5. Poll owner responsibility

Poll creators are responsible for writing clear questions, sharing poll keys with the intended audience, and interpreting results in the proper context.

6. Availability

We work to keep the service available, but downtime, maintenance, hosting limits, network issues, or database problems may affect access.

7. Changes

We may update these terms as the product changes. Continued use of Votogram after updates means you accept the revised terms.
